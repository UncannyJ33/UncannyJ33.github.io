SJ Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

SJ Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

The app accesses Google Calendar data — event titles, times, and calendars — solely to read and manage the owner's own calendar on his behalf.

All data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. No data from any other user is collected, because the app has no other users.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
